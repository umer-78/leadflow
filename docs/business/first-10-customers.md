# First 10 Customers Playbook ($0 Capital)

## Days 1 to 14: Customer #1 ($1,000 MRR)
- Select 10 top-rated local cosmetic dental clinics running Google Ads.
- Run the After-Hours Audit: inspect evening contact form leakage.
- Generate customized demo in LeadFlow AI with their exact procedure names and prices.
- Record a 90-second Loom showing their custom AI receptionist answering their treatment FAQs.
- Send personalized outreach to practice owner/director.
- Offer 14-day trial with setup fee deferred until first 5 consultations booked.

## Days 15 to 45: Customers #2 - #5 ($5,000 MRR)
- Leverage Customer #1 case study: document reduction in lead response time (from 4 hours to 45 seconds).
- Present actual numbers: "Apex Smile Studio captured 12 qualified after-hours leads in their first 3 weeks."
- Close 4 more practices on the Growth plan ($1,000/mo).

## Days 46 to 90: Customers #6 - #10 ($10,000 MRR)
- Activate Doctor-to-Doctor Referral program.
- Expand outreach into adjacent facial plastic surgery and high-ticket orthodontic practices.
- Scale to 10 active clients: $10,000 MRR ($120,000 ARR).

## Days 91 to 180: Path to $20,000 MRR
- Maintain < 2% monthly churn with proactive weekly health audits.
- Upsell multi-location practices to the Pro plan ($2,000/mo).
- 20 active practices on Growth tier ($1,000/mo) or 10 practices on Pro tier ($2,000/mo) achieves the **$20,000/month recurring revenue target**.
