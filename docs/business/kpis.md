# Weekly Operations & KPI Tracking

## Primary Agency Operating Metrics
1. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): Target $20,000.
2. Active Paying Clients: Target 20 on Growth ($1,000/mo) or 10 on Pro ($2,000/mo).
3. MRR Gap: $20,000 - Current MRR.
4. Clients Needed: ceil(MRR Gap / Average Client MRR).
5. Monthly Lead Volume Handled: Total leads captured across all tenant clinics.
6. Qualification Rate: % of captured leads meeting HIGH or MEDIUM qualification tier.
7. Consultation Conversion: % of qualified leads converted to scheduled clinic appointments.
8. Client Churn: Target < 2% monthly.
