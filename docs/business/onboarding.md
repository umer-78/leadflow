# 11-Step Onboarding Protocol

1. Profile: Practice legal name, primary phone, location, and doctor credentials.
2. Services: Core 3-5 procedures with starting prices or price ranges.
3. FAQs: Common patient concerns (financing, insurance, discomfort, recovery).
4. Pricing & Financing: Clarify CareCredit, Sunbit, or in-house payment options.
5. Hours: Operating schedule and after-hours triage window.
6. Staff Contacts: Duty receptionist email and SMS numbers for urgent alerts.
7. Appointment Procedures: Consultation duration and provider clinician names.
8. Knowledge Document Ingestion: Clinical brochure and procedure guidelines chunked and indexed.
9. AI Receptionist Persona: Custom greeting, brand color, and triage questions.
10. Test Mode: Verification of medical refusal, pricing accuracy, and lead capture.
11. Production Activation: Embed snippet copied to website `<head>` or `<body>`.
