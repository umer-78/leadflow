Convert qualified discovery calls into signed, paid retainers ($500 Starter, $1,000 Growth, $2,000 Pro + setup fees).

## 2. Proposal Structure
- **Executive Summary**: Identified revenue leakage from missed after-hours patient inquiries.

- **Financial Scope**: Setup fee + Monthly recurring retainer.
- **Implementation Timeline**: 72-hour onboarding and live widget deployment.
- **Owner Review & Approval**: Generated via Owner Command Center with 1-click human approval before transmission.
