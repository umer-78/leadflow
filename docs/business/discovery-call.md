# Discovery Call Framework (15 Minutes)

## Objective
Uncover the financial cost of missed inquiries and front-desk triage friction.

## Core Questions
1. "How many website visitors or inquiries does your practice receive each month across Google Ads and your website?"
2. "What percentage of those inquiries come in between 5 PM and 8 AM or over the weekend?"
3. "When a patient asks for a high-value procedure like Veneers or Implants after hours, how long before someone on your team calls them back?"
4. "What is your average revenue for an accepted full-case treatment?"
5. "If we recovered just 2 to 3 consultations that normally bounce to another local practice every month, what would that mean for your monthly collections?"
