# Sales Process & Conversion Pipeline

## Pipeline Stages
1. **PROSPECT**: Practice identified with public contact info.
2. **RESEARCHED**: AI Research Assistant generated verified observations and outreach angle.
3. **CONTACTED**: Personalized 4-sentence outreach approved by human operator and sent.
4. **REPLIED**: Practice owner or director requested preview link or more information.
5. **DEMO**: 10-minute interactive simulation configured with practice's exact procedures.
6. **DISCOVERY**: Diagnostic call uncovering monthly lost revenue from inquiry leakage.
7. **PROPOSAL**: 1-click Proposal (Starter $500/mo, Growth $1,000/mo, Pro $2,000/mo) sent.
8. **NEGOTIATION**: Clarifying contract length (month-to-month or quarterly) and onboarding date.
9. **WON**: Payment collected via Stripe/card; 11-step onboarding launched.
10. **LOST**: Reason documented (e.g. clinic already uses proprietary EMR chat).
