# Client Success, Support, Retention & Referrals

## Client Health Score Model (Transparent & Explainable)
- **GREEN**: Weekly lead intake normal, sub-60s AI responses, 0 unanswered handoffs, automations active.
- **YELLOW**: Lead volume down > 25% from 30-day baseline, or staff taking > 24 hours to confirm booked consultation slots.
- **RED**: Automation error logged, or clinic unhandled emergency handoffs pending > 4 hours. Triggers mandatory proactive agency check-in.

## Retention Cadence
- **Day 7**: First-week check-in (review captured leads and staff notification speed).
- **Day 30**: Monthly ROI Review (show total captured leads, qualified leads, booked appointments, and calculated estimated revenue).
- **Day 60**: Knowledge base update (add new seasonal procedures or updated fee guides).

## Referral Engine
- Every satisfied clinic owner is invited to introduce 1-2 non-competing colleagues in adjacent territories.
- Offer $500 billing credit or charitable donation on behalf of the referring doctor upon new practice activation.
