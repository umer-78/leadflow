# Competitors and feature parity

What dental practices can buy instead of LeadFlow, what those products do,
and where LeadFlow stands. Researched from public information (vendor pages,
review directories and comparison sites) in October 2026. Vendor claims and
third-party prices are **not independently verified**: check a vendor's own
site before quoting a number to a prospect.

## Who we compete with

**Patient-communication suites.** Broad platforms sold to the whole front desk.
- **Weave** started as a VoIP phone system and added texting, reminders and
  recall on top. Its phone system is bundled into the price.
- **NexHealth** started as online booking and a patient portal. It now covers
  forms, reminders, insurance verification and messaging. One third-party
  review lists about $450/month (communication) and $700/month (full package)
  for one location.
- Demandforce, Solutionreach and RevenueWell play in the same space.

**AI receptionists for dental.** Closer to what LeadFlow does.
- **Arini** answers calls and texts and books into the practice-management
  system. It has no fixed public price. Directories list figures from about
  $499/month, and some say pricing is custom.
- Dentina and Annie are other examples.
- Generic voice-AI platforms such as Retell AI are also used to build dental
  receptionists.

## Where LeadFlow is ahead

- **Privacy by default.** Out of the box, patients' messages never leave the
  clinic's server. Claude answers are an opt-in, with contact details removed
  first, and emergencies, booking and prices always stay on the server.
- **Answers you can check.** Clinical answers come from a researched library
  in which every article cites its sources. A clinic can reword any article,
  and its own wording is never overwritten.
- **Lead pipeline built in.** Competitors remind existing patients. LeadFlow
  also turns website visitors into scored leads and follows them up.
- **Measured quality.** A 333-case quality bench runs on every change. The
  AI Center shows each clinic its own unanswered questions and corrections,
  so the gaps get filled.

## Gaps worth closing next

Ranked by what prospects ask about and what moves results:

1. **SMS.** Practices expect reminders by text. Add a Twilio channel next to
   email for reminders, waitlist offers and recalls, with opt-out handling.
2. **Practice-management integration.** Start with Open Dental, which has a
   public API. Recalls and reactivation would then use real visit history
   instead of LeadFlow-only appointments.
3. **Phone answering.** Put a voice front end on the same engine, so
   after-hours calls get the same answers as chat.
4. **Intake forms** sent with the booking confirmation.
