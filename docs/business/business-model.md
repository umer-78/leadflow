# LeadFlow AI - Business Operations Manual

## 1. Business Model & $20k/Mo Architecture
Target: $20,000/month recurring revenue ($240,000 ARR).
Model: High-ticket lead capture, qualification, and appointment automation for Dental & Cosmetic clinics.
Pricing:
- Starter: $500/mo + $500 setup
- Growth: $1,000/mo + $1,000 setup (Target: 20 clients = $20,000 MRR)
- Pro: $2,000/mo + $2,000 setup (Target: 10 clients = $20,000 MRR)

## 2. Zero-Capital Methodology ($0 Startup Capital)
- Free local components and AI Gateway abstraction.
- Gemini API free tier / Mock deterministic engine during development.
- In-memory & local-first tenant persistence.
- Local test runners and zero paid dependencies.
