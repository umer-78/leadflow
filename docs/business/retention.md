# LeadFlow AI — Client Retention & Health Scoring Manual

## 1. Client Health Score Architecture
- **GREEN**: Weekly lead intake > 5, 0 automation errors, active consultation booking rate > 15%.
- **YELLOW**: Lead activity declining by 20% week-over-week. Triggers automated check-in.
- **RED**: Zero interactions or automation failure. Triggers immediate owner intervention.

## 2. Retention Tactics
- Monthly ROI Summary Reports demonstrating captured revenue.
- Proactive seasonal campaign automations.
