# Demo & Proposal Process

## The 7-Minute Demo Script
1. Open the LeadFlow AI widget tailored with the prospect's real practice name and procedures.
2. Show immediate answers to common questions (financing, hours, procedure length).
3. Demonstrate clinical guardrail (ask for medical diagnosis -> AI refuses and requests in-clinic visit).
4. Demonstrate instant lead qualification: visitor leaves phone number -> AI scores lead HIGH and triggers SMS alert to staff.
5. Show the Client Dashboard where the new lead and consultation queue update in real time.

## Proposal Rules
- Never send complex 20-page PDFs.
- Send a clear 1-page proposal generated from `/admin/prospects` detailing:
  - Setup fee ($500 / $1,000 / $2,000)
  - Monthly fee ($500 / $1,000 / $2,000)
  - Deliverables & SLA
  - 14-day proposal validity
