What it does

An intake machine, not a chatbot

Four jobs a front desk does, running around the clock and tenant-isolated per clinic.

Answers, within bounds

A 24/7 receptionist held to each clinic's verified guidelines. It never fabricates pricing and refuses to give a medical diagnosis, so it protects the practice while it captures the lead.

Scores intent, with reasons

Every enquiry is sorted into high, medium or low intent with a plain-language rationale, so the team calls the patients worth calling first instead of guessing from a full inbox.

Follows up, then stops

A structured sequence reaches out over the first week and halts the moment the patient replies, books, or opts out. No clinic ever nags a patient who already said yes or no.

Books and logs everything

It offers real consultation slots, confirms them, and keeps an audit log of every message, so the practice has a record and the owner can see exactly what was said.

Why this lead, and why now

The score is never a bare number. It comes with the reasons behind it, so the team can trust it and act on it.

High intent

Named a high-value procedure, asked about timing or availability, and left a reachable contact. Call first.

Medium intent

Clear interest but missing a detail, such as timing or budget readiness. Worth a prompt, well-timed follow-up.

Low intent

Early-stage or information-only. Nurtured gently, never pushed, and never allowed to crowd out the ready patients.

Questions

Before you deploy

How does it avoid saying the wrong thing?

The assistant is constrained to each clinic's own guidelines. It will not quote prices it cannot stand behind and it refuses to give medical advice or a diagnosis, deferring those to the clinician at the consultation. Every message is logged so the practice can review what was said.

Does it work for more than one clinic?

Yes. It is multi-tenant by construction: each practice's leads, calendar and knowledge base are isolated from every other, and that isolation is checked by an in-app test runner.

What does it cost to run?

It is designed to run on free tiers: Google Gemini's free prototyping tier for the assistant, and a free-tier host and database. The repository includes a step-by-step zero-cost deployment guide.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. LeadFlow AI is open source. The repository ships a Dockerfile and deployment configuration, so you can run it on your own host with your own keys.

Is patient data handled responsibly?

Conversations are stored per clinic for the follow-up sequence and the audit log, isolated between tenants. See the Privacy Policy for what is kept and for how long. Treat any clinical or payment workflow as subject to your local healthcare rules.
